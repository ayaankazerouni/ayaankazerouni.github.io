Read the introductions of the following papers:

- [Breaking Through Binaries: Compiler-quality Instrumentation for Better Binary-only Fuzzing](https://www.cs.utah.edu/~snagy/papers/21SEC.pdf) (Nagy et al.)
- [Exploiting Input Sanitization for Regex Denial of Service](https://docs.lib.purdue.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1179&context=ecepubs) (Barlas, Du, & Davis)

For each paper, write a bulleted list of short statements. The list should contain:

- Context
- Prior work in the area
- Gaps in prior work
- What the paper does to address the gaps
- Results, if applicable

You don't have to write *exactly* 5 bullet points---any of the items above may need multiple bullet points.
