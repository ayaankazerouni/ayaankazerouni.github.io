Hi, I'm Ayaan! I'm an Associate Professor of Computer Science at Cal Poly.

My research in Computing Education focuses on improving student self-regulation in software development (e.g., testing, debugging, help-seeking), fostering motivation to learn computing through autonomy and culturally relevant pedagogy, and expanding opportunities to learn through service.

For more details, visit my [research]({{site.baseurl}}/research) and [teaching]({{site.baseurl}}/teaching) pages, or see [master's theses]({{site.baseurl}}/masters-theses) I have supervised.

I also advise the Cal Poly chapter of [Hack4Impact](https://calpoly.hack4impact.org/) and the [CSSE student tutoring centre](https://csc.calpoly.edu/tutoring/).

## About me

I obtained my PhD in Computer Science at [Virginia Tech](https://cs.vt.edu) in 2020, under the joint advisement of [Cliff Shaffer](https://people.cs.vt.edu/~shaffer) and [Steve Edwards](https://people.cs.vt.edu/~edwards/).
Before that I obtained a BS in Computer Science at the [University of West Georgia](https://www.westga.edu/) in 2015.

I'm originally from Mumbai, India.

I like [playing](https://www.youtube.com/@ayaankazerouni) and listening to folk music, reading fiction and non-fiction, hiking, cycling and a good TV binge.
